================================================================================
  METHOD 1: GOOGLE DRIVE WEBSITE (Easiest)
================================================================================

Step 1: Browser mein jao → https://drive.google.com
Step 2: Login karo apne Google account se
Step 3: "New" button click karo → "Folder upload"
Step 4: "jubelay-google-drive" folder select karo
Step 5: Upload complete hone ka wait karo

DOCX files ko Google Docs mein convert karne ke liye:
  → File par right-click → "Open with" → "Google Docs"

================================================================================
  METHOD 2: ZIP UPLOAD (Faster)
================================================================================

Step 1: "jubelay-google-drive.zip" file upload karo Google Drive par
Step 2: Zip par right-click → "Open with" → "Google Drive" (extract)
Step 3: Ya download karke extract karo locally

================================================================================
  METHOD 3: MOBILE (Phone se)
================================================================================

Step 1: Zip file apne phone par bhejo (WhatsApp/email)
Step 2: Google Drive app kholo
Step 3: "+" button → "Upload" → zip file select karo
Step 4: Extract karo Drive app mein

================================================================================
  GOOGLE DOCS CONVERSION
================================================================================

.docx files Google Drive par upload karne ke baad:

1. File par double-click karo
2. "Open with Google Docs" select karo
3. Ab yeh Google Doc ban jayega — edit, share, collaborate kar sakte ho

Images (.png) directly Google Drive par preview hongi — convert ki zaroorat nahi.
