# JUBELAY — Brand Guidelines
### Visual Identity & Design Reference

---

## LOGO

### Primary Logo
- **Wordmark:** JUBELAY
- **Font:** Playfair Display, Bold
- **Color:** #2C2C2C (JUBELAY Black)
- **Tagline:** "Style Made Simple" — Assistant font, #8B8178

### Logo Variations

| Version | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| Primary | JUBELAY black on white |
| Reversed | JUBELAY white on dark backgrounds |
| Favicon | "J" in gold (#C9A96E) on black square |

### Logo Don'ts
- Don't stretch or distort
- Don't change colors
- Don't add shadows
- Minimum width: 120px
